# Arrival and personal Orbit — October 12, 2026

The homepage now opens with an original live POV sequence based on the user’s 1.89-second Apple reference clip. It reveals the existing room through an expanding lens, brings a curved glass plane toward the center, and dissolves into the existing Home window and dock in 3.9 seconds. No Apple footage or third-party music is shipped. The sequence is responsive CSS with perspective; the curvature is a visual illusion rather than WebXR or a physically curved DOM mesh. Normal navigation, room panning, and the portfolio remain unchanged after arrival.

The opening is purely visual: no captions, names, tagline, dark vertical lines, decorative circular strokes, Skip intro, or sound controls. The pale green warped plane flows directly into the real Home window. Sound is enabled by default. Browser autoplay policy may keep a fresh visitor’s opening silent; the first trusted interaction unlocks audio, and Replay arrival then plays with its cue. The automatic arrival chord lasts 2.4 seconds and ends before three seconds. Escape or Tab can finish the visual sequence without adding a visible control. Hidden tabs, route changes, and reduced motion also end it. Deep links bypass it. Enlarged text uses a scrolling page. The boot-time fallback and no-JavaScript page still work.

`spatial-audio.js` synthesizes an original glass/pop click inspired by spatial UI; it does not include a sampled Apple system sound. One AudioContext and one cached 85 ms mono buffer serve all interactions. Trusted clicks inside the content windows, dock, header, guide, room controls, and dialogs get the cue. Pointer drags stay quiet. Burst duplicates, late autoplay unlocks, hidden tabs, and unavailable audio are handled without queued or unexpected playback. Audio playback does not replace visual feedback.

The status bar has equal-height availability and Siri pills. The 100% battery is now a decorative full-charge indicator with an accurate tooltip and screen-reader description. It does not request device battery status. Look around and Recenter share width, icon size, padding, and label alignment. Siri’s existing motion is retained; its shell, visor and ring now use cream, sage and olive tones.
