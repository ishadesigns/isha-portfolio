# Arrival and personal Orbit — October 12, 2026

Welcome now appears directly, with no intermediate warped glass pane. A brief 1.05-second dark headset reveal uses a horizontal oval on both its inner opening and outer silhouette. The outer element has a 50% border radius, so its scaled edges cannot form a sharp rectangle. Navigation is available immediately; the room image no longer needs to decode before the reveal begins. No Apple footage or third-party music is shipped.

The welcome cue resets and attempts playback on every document load, including refreshes and direct links. It lasts about one second and runs independently of the visual reveal. Browser autoplay permission cannot be inferred from local storage or carried across page reloads by the app. If audio is blocked, the first trusted pointer/key interaction makes a fresh cue attempt, once per visit; stale requests cannot overwrite a successful one. Replay arrival explicitly replays both the brief reveal and cue. No visible sound or skip controls are added. Keyboard, hidden-tab, and reduced-motion behavior remain supported.

`spatial-audio.js` synthesizes an original glass/pop click inspired by spatial UI; it does not include a sampled Apple system sound. One AudioContext and one cached 85 ms mono buffer serve all interactions. Trusted clicks inside the content windows, dock, header, guide, room controls, and dialogs get the cue. Pointer drags stay quiet. Burst duplicates, late autoplay unlocks, hidden tabs, and unavailable audio are handled without queued or unexpected playback. Audio playback does not replace visual feedback.

The status bar has equal-height availability and Siri pills. The 100% battery is now a decorative full-charge indicator with an accurate tooltip and screen-reader description. It does not request device battery status. Look around and Recenter share width, icon size, padding, and label alignment. Siri’s existing motion is retained; its shell, visor and ring now use cream, sage and olive tones.
